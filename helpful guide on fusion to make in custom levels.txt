

=============================
MY GUIDE ON HOW TO DO THE FUSIONS
=============================

ARMOR PUMPKIN

STEP ONE:
Get a Joker pumpkin

get a magnet-shroom and a pumpkin to fuse together
=
lure pumpkin

get that lure pumpkin and fuse it with a jack in a box
=
joker pumpkin


STEP TWO:
Get a Cherry pumpkin

get a cherry bomb and fuse it with a pumpkin
=
Cherry pumpkin

STEP THREE:
Fusing all of them

get the plants in this order:
Joker pumpkin + magnet-shroom + cherry pumpkin

and put a jicamagicker on the magnet-shroom



GATLING PEA TURRET

STEP ONE:
Get a repeater

fuse a peashooter with another peashooter. and do that again.
=
repeater

STEP TWO:
Fusing all of them

get the plants in this order:
repeater + threepeater + repeater

and put a jicamagicker on the threepeater



GIANT CHOMPER

its literally just 3 chompers together with a jicamagicker in the middle over a chomper


OBSIDIAN TALL-NUT


STEP ONE:
Get a Glacier tall-nut

get a tall-nut and freeze it with a ice-shroom
=
Glacier tall-nut

STEP TWO:
Get a Flame tall-nut

get a tall-nut and burn it with a jalapeno
=
Flame tall-nut

STEP THREE:
Fusing them together

just fuse them together bro... its that easy.
