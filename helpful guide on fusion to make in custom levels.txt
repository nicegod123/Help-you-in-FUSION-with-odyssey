=============================
MY GUIDE ON HOW TO DO THE FUSIONS
=============================

ARMOR PUMPKIN

STEP ONE:
Get a Joker pumpkin

get a magnet-shroom and a pumpkin to fuse together
=
lure pumpkin

kill a jack in a box zombie to get a jack in a box

get a lure pumpkin and fuse it with a jack in a box
=
joker pumpkin


STEP TWO:
Get a Cherry pumpkin

get a cherry bomb and fuse it with a pumpkin
=
Cherry pumpkin

STEP THREE:
Fusing all of them

get the plants in this order:
Joker pumpkin + magnet-shroom + cherry pumpkin

and put a jicamagicker on the magnet-shroom



GATLING PEA TURRET

STEP ONE:
Get a repeater

fuse a peashooter with another peashooter. and do that again.
=
repeater

STEP TWO:
Fusing all of them

get the plants in this order:
repeater + threepeater + repeater

and put a jicamagicker on the threepeater



GIANT CHOMPER

its literally just 3 chompers together with a jicamagicker in the middle over a chomper


OBSIDIAN TALL-NUT


STEP ONE:
Get a Glacier tall-nut

get a tall-nut and freeze it with a ice-shroom
=
Glacier tall-nut

STEP TWO:
Get a Flame tall-nut

get a tall-nut and burn it with a jalapeno
=
Flame tall-nut

STEP THREE:
Fusing them together

just fuse them together bro... its that easy.



OBLIVION-SHROOM

STEP ONE:
get a doomicle-shroom

get a fume-shroom and fuse it with a ice-shroom
=
frost-shroom

fuse the frost-shroom with a doom-shroom
=
doomicle-shroom


STEP TWO:
get a cryoom-shroom

get a gloom-shroom and fuse it with a ice-shroom
=
cryoom-shroom

STEP THREE:
fusing them together

yeah just fuse teh cryoom-shroom with a doomicle-shroom
=
OBLIVION-SHROOM



DOOMINATOR-SHROOM

STEP ONE:
get a doomicle-shroom

get a fume-shroom and fuse it with a ice-shroom
=
frost-shroom

fuse the ice-shroom with a doom-shroom
=
doomicle-shroom

STEP TWO:
get a cryonic-shroom

fuse together a hypno-shroom and a ice-shroom
=
cryonic-shroom


STEP THREE:
fuse them together

get the doomicle-shroom and fuse it with teh cryonic-shroom
=
DOOMINATOR-SHROOM




VORTEX MELON

STEP ONE:
get a winter melon

fuse melon pult and ice-shroom
=
winter melon

STEP TWO:
get a chrono melon

kill a chronoporter zombie to get the chrono-disk

fuse a melon pult with a magnet-shroom
=
melon lure

get the chrono-disk and fuse it with the melon lure
=
chrono melon

STEP THREE:
combining/fusing

fuse the winter melon and the chrono melon
=
vortex melon


LASER PUMPKIN

STEP ONE:
get a carrier pumpkin

(requires to beat the challenge "Carrier Pumpkin Fusion Challenge (8)")

fuse a cactus and pumpkin
=
thorn pumpkin

fuse the thorn pumpkin and a blover
=
carrier pumpkin


STEP TWO:
get a magneto blover

fuse a magnet-shroom with a blover
=
magneto blover

STEP THREE:
fuse them together

fuse the carrier pumpkin with the magneto blover
=
laser pumpkin

(further is blast pumpkin)

EXTRA STEP:
fusing some more!

fuse the laser pumpkin with a cherry bomb
=
blast pumpkin
